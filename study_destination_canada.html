Study in Canada as a Bangladeshi Student

Canada is a top-tier destination for international students, with 31 universities featured in the latest QS World University Rankings. Its status as one of the safest and most welcoming countries, alongside its multicultural society, helps students adjust easily. Toronto, Montreal, Vancouver, Ottawa, and Quebec City are among the most popular cities.

Canada offers a diverse range of study choices for international students—from popular fields like Computer Science (including AI and Data Science), Engineering, and Business Management (including MBA), to unique and growing disciplines like Environmental Science, Animation and Gaming, and International Relations. Programs emphasize both cutting-edge research and practical, hands-on learning experiences, often through co-op and Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) opportunities.

FAQs

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

How much does it cost to study in Canada?

Costs vary depending on the program, but scholarships are available to reduce financial burdens.

Can I work while studying in Canada?

Yes, students can work up to 20 hours per week during term and full-time during breaks.

What are the language requirements for studying in Canada?

Most universities require an English proficiency test like IELTS or TOEFL. Some may also accept French tests for certain programs.
