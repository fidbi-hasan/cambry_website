Why Choose the USA for Study?

The United States is one of the most popular destinations for international students, offering world-class education through its more than 4,000 universities—including prestigious Ivy League institutions—and consistently ranking among the top globally for academic excellence. Students benefit from diverse learning opportunities, such as cutting-edge research facilities and flexible curriculums that promote unmatched academic freedom.

The country also provides exceptional global networking prospects, allowing students to connect with peers and faculty from around the world and build lifelong professional relationships. Additionally, post-graduation opportunities like Optional Practical Training (OPT) and Curricular Practical Training (CPT) enable international students to gain valuable work experience in their field.

FAQs

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

How much does it cost to study in USA?

The cost varies based on your course and location. On average, tuition fees range from AUD 20,000 to 45,000 per year, while living expenses can be around AUD 21,000 per year.

Can I work while studying in USA?

Yes! International students can work up to 48 hours per fortnight during semesters and full-time during breaks.

Do I need health insurance to study in USA?

Yes, all international students must purchase OSHC (Overseas Student Health Cover) to access healthcare services while studying in USA.
