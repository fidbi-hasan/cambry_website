FAQ

How long does it take to get a response?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. If your query is urgent, please call us directly at the number provided.

What should I bring for a consultation?

For your initial consultation, please bring your passport, academic transcripts, language proficiency scores (like IELTS/TOEFL), and any previous visa application documents, if applicable.

Is there a fee for the initial consultation?

No, the initial consultation and assessment are completely free. We will discuss your profile and provide you with the best options available to you.
