Study in New Zealand as a Bangladeshi Student

New Zealand is an increasingly desirable destination for international students, celebrated for its world-class education within a safe, supportive, and stunning natural environment. Its institutions are committed to high academic standards, with all eight of its universities consistently placed in the QS World University Rankings. The country's welcoming culture and high quality of life help students adjust easily. Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, and Dunedin are among the most popular cities.

New Zealand offers a diverse range of study choices—from popular fields like Information Technology, Engineering, and Business Management, to unique and growing disciplines like Agriculture, Environmental Science, and Tourism & Hospitality Management. Programs often feature a hands-on learning approach through internships, placements, and practical projects, preparing students for the global workforce and leading to post-study work opportunities.

Obtain Offer of Place

Obtain Offer of Place from an approved New Zealand education provider (must be a signatory to the Code of Practice).

2

Gather Documents

Gather Documents including your Offer of Place, proof of sufficient funds (for tuition and living costs: NZD $20,000/year for students over 17), passport, and accepted English test scores.

↓
3

Arrange Health & Character Checks

Arrange Health & Character Checks by booking any necessary medical exams (chest X-ray or medical certificate) and applying for police certificates (if required).

↓
4

Submit Application

Submit Application for the Fee Paying Student Visa (the most common type) online via the Immigration New Zealand website and pay the non-refundable fee.

↓
5

Await Decision

Await Decision which may involve a request for further information or an interview (if Immigration New Zealand requires one to verify your application details or genuine intent to study).

Scholarship and Financial Aids

Fund Your Education in New Zealand

Manaaki New Zealand Scholarships

A fully-funded scholarship from the New Zealand Government, covering tuition fees, living allowance (stipend), and travel for eligible students from selected developing countries (including many in the Asia-Pacific region) for undergraduate and postgraduate study.

New Zealand Commonwealth Scholarship

New Zealand Commonwealth Scholarship For master’s and PhD students from Commonwealth countries (excluding New Zealand and Australia) to support their studies in areas that contribute to the development needs of their home country.

University-Specific Scholarships

Institutions offer numerous awards, such as the University of Auckland International Student Excellence Scholarship (up to NZD $10,000 for high achievers) and the Victoria University of Wellington Tongarewa Scholarship for academic merit.

New Zealand Excellence Awards (NZEA)

New Zealand Excellence Awards (NZEA) Merit-based scholarships designed to attract high-achieving international students to study postgraduate degrees in various fields at New Zealand universities.

Post-Study Opportunities

New Zealand offers excellent opportunities for international graduates

Post-Study Work Visa

🇳🇿 Apply for a Post Study Work Visa to stay and work for up to 3 years (duration depends on the level of your qualification). For a Level 7 Bachelor's degree or higher, you can work for any employer in almost any job.

Buildup Demanding Career

Explore career paths in high-demand sectors such as Healthcare (Registered Nurses, Specialists), Technology (ICT specialists, Software Developers), and Engineering (Civil, Mechanical), many of which are listed on the Green List.

Permanent Residency

Apply for a Straight to Residence Visa (Tier 1 roles) or a Work to Residence Visa (Tier 2 roles) after securing a job with an accredited employer.
